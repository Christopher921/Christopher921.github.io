When Contacting Support

Pro Upgrade and Purchases

The Pro upgrade is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is processed by Apple through the App Store.

If you have already purchased Pro, open the Pro tab and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Pro can be restored on any device signed in with that Apple ID.

Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

About the App

IncomeTaxCalculator - Canada provides estimates of Canadian income tax, payroll deductions and take-home pay. The calculations are estimates and may differ from actual tax liabilities or payroll deductions.

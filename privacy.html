Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Canadian Take-Home Pay ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Information You Enter

The App allows you to enter financial information such as employment income, bonuses, benefits, investment income, deductions and other amounts in order to calculate estimated Canadian taxes and take-home pay.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, mailing address, telephone number or email address in order to use the calculator.

Information entered into the calculator is used to perform calculations and display results within the App.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads is a third-party advertising service.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising information, performance and diagnostic information, and information about interactions with advertisements and the App. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, diagnostics and improving the advertising service.

For more information about Google's handling of information, please review Google's privacy documentation.

3. Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law or Apple's platform policies, advertising-related permissions or consent choices may be presented to users.

You can also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your device's iOS settings.

4. Data Storage

The App does not require an online account to calculate estimated tax and take-home pay.

The developer does not intentionally collect or maintain a database of the financial amounts that you enter into the calculator.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party software and services necessary to provide advertising and related functionality. These third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements or third-party services. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
